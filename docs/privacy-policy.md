# HisaabMate Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 29 September 2026

HisaabMate is a local-first personal finance app developed by **Sagar Haridas Kalel**. It helps you record borrowing, lending, expenses, shared spending, group expenses, and settlements.

## What data the app handles

Depending on the features you use, HisaabMate may store the following on your device:

- Ledger records, expenses, split expenses, and settlement details.
- Contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, and UPI IDs that you enter or choose to use.
- Contact profile photos selected from your device or taken with the camera.
- App preferences such as language and theme.

## Where your data is stored

HisaabMate stores this information locally on your device using local app storage. The current version does not require an account and has no developer-operated backend, cloud database, analytics, advertising, or cloud synchronization.

We do not collect or transmit your personal or financial data to our servers.

## Contacts, camera, and photos

The app requests contacts access only when you choose a contact-related feature. Contacts are used to help create or match ledger contacts; HisaabMate does not modify your device contacts.

Camera and photo-library access are optional. They are used only when you choose to take or select a contact profile photo.

## Backups and sharing

When you choose Export Data, HisaabMate creates a backup containing your records, contact details, UPI IDs, and saved contact photos. The backup is saved to a location selected by you through the operating system’s file picker.

You may also share a backup through the operating system share sheet. Once you share a file, the receiving app, service, or person may store or access it according to their own policies. Share backups only with destinations and people you trust.

Backups are not automatically uploaded to HisaabMate or deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the app. You are responsible for managing copies you save or share.

## UPI and external apps

UPI actions open compatible external UPI applications installed on your device. HisaabMate does not process, hold, or access your payment funds. Any payment activity is handled by the external UPI application and its provider.

## Your choices and deletion

You can:

- Use the app without creating an account.
- Deny optional contacts, camera, or photo-library permissions.
- Export your data when you choose.
- Import a backup, which replaces the current app records after confirmation.
- Clear app data from the app’s settings.
- Remove app-local data by uninstalling HisaabMate.

Exported or shared backup files are separate copies and are not removed automatically when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

## Security note

The current version is designed as a local-first app and does not provide cloud security, account recovery, database encryption, or encrypted backup files. Protect your device and treat exported backups as sensitive files.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app behavior changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published.
